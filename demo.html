About the kiosk

Reliv Smart Health Kiosk is a self-service screening station from Reliv Medicare Private Limited in Kolkata, India. Core checks include blood pressure, SpO₂, pulse, temperature, height and weight, with calculated BMI. Optional modules and report access depend on configuration.

A typical guided session is around three minutes; the time varies with the selected checks and user assistance. This demonstration video is shorter than a complete session and does not establish a fixed completion time. Screening supports health awareness and is not a diagnosis.
