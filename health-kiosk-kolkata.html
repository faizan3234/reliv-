Reliv vs. "100+ parameter" health kiosks

Some Indian health kiosks advertise 100+ parameters. Those machines usually add blood, urine, ECG or lipid panels, and many of the "parameters" are calculated from a smaller number of real measurements. Reliv takes a different approach: a simple, guided, non-invasive check you can do in minutes inside your campus or community.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a health kiosk or Health ATM in Kolkata?

Yes. Reliv has health-screening kiosk stations in Kolkata at IEM Gurukul Campus (Sector V, Bidhannagar), St. James' School and Eco Park (New Town). Because stations sit inside partner venues, please confirm access and operating hours before you travel.

What does the Reliv health kiosk check?

Blood pressure, SpO₂ (oxygen saturation), pulse, body temperature (contactless), height, weight and calculated BMI. An optional eyesight screening may be available depending on the kiosk configuration. A typical session takes about 3 minutes.

Is Reliv a 100+ parameter health kiosk?

No. Reliv is an everyday, non-invasive vital-signs screening kiosk. Machines advertised with 100+ parameters usually add blood, urine, ECG or lipid panels and often count calculated values as parameters. Reliv does not take blood samples and does not claim lab-grade panels.

Can Reliv diagnose a medical condition?

No. Reliv gives screening information for awareness only. Discuss concerning readings or symptoms with a qualified healthcare professional.

Are Smart Health Kiosk and Health ATM different products?

No. "Smart Health Kiosk" and "Health ATM" are two names for the same self-service screening station.

Which languages does the Reliv kiosk support?

English, Hindi and Bengali, with on-screen and voice guidance.

Is the Reliv kiosk free? How do I get my report?

Access and report options depend on the kiosk configuration. Any payment or activation step is shown on the kiosk before you continue. The companion app is available at reliv7.vercel.app.

Can a college, school, office or gym get a Reliv kiosk?

Yes. Reliv works with institutions on purchase, lease or revenue-share options. Use the enquiry form on the For Organisations page or email relivcustomercare.in@gmail.com.
