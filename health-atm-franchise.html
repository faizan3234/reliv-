Frequently Asked Questions — Health ATM Franchise

How can I start a Health ATM business in India?

Starting a Health ATM business with Reliv requires minimal upfront friction. You can choose to: (1) Buy the machine outright for ₹95,000 only and retain 100% of screening revenue; (2) Lease a kiosk at ₹12,000/month flat rent with zero CapEx; or (3) Apply for Space & Profit Sharing if you manage a high-footfall location where Reliv deploys the kiosk at ₹0 hardware cost.

How much can a Health ATM owner earn per month?

At a conservative screening fee of ₹30 per checkup and a modest footfall of 20 users per day, a kiosk generates ₹18,000 per month (₹2,16,000 per year) pure net profit for an outright owner, recovering the entire machine cost in approximately 5.3 months. Additional income is generated via digital advertising on the idle display screen and automated health kit dispensing margins.

What space and power infrastructure is required for a Health ATM?

A Reliv Health ATM requires only a 4×4 sq. ft. floor footprint (16 sq. ft.) and a standard single-phase 220V electrical socket. The machine comes with built-in telemetry, cellular connectivity, and requires no water line or clinical drainage.

Does a Health ATM require an attendant, operator, or nurse?

No. Reliv kiosks are 100% autonomous. The touchscreen interface and multilingual Voice AI assist users in English, Hindi, and Bengali step-by-step through every check, eliminating operator salaries and staffing overhead.
