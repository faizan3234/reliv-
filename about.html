WHY RELIV MATTERS

Turning Healthcare from Reactive Illness to Proactive Wellness

In India, millions suffer from undetected hypertension, cardiovascular stress, obesity, and metabolic imbalances simply because visiting clinics requires scheduling, travel, needles, and high costs. Reliv brings preventive health to where life happens.

⚡

3-Minute Non-Invasive Screening

Users step up to a Reliv Health Kiosk and complete 6+ vital measurements—including Blood Pressure, SpO₂, Pulse Rate, Body Temperature, Height, Visual Acuity, and BMI—in under 180 seconds with zero needles, pain, or wait times.

🗣️

Inclusive Multi-Lingual Guidance

Engineered for India's linguistic diversity. The kiosk talks and guides users in their mother tongue—supporting English, Hindi, and Bengali—with friendly, voice-guided instructions that anyone can navigate independently.

📲

Instant Digital Health Card

No paperwork or lost slips. Within seconds of test completion, a secure QR code sends an easy-to-read, color-coded health summary directly to the user's mobile via web browser and WhatsApp, ready to save or share.

🤖

Intelligent AI Health Coach

Beyond raw numbers, the integrated Reliv AI Health Coach translates complex clinical metrics into understandable, actionable wellness recommendations—monitoring longitudinal trends to spot warning signs early.

📍

Placed Where People Live & Study

Instead of requiring hospital visits, Reliv brings clinical-grade preventive hardware right into daily footfall hubs: university campuses, corporate tech parks, metro stations, residential societies, and athletic centers.

🏥

Reliv Verified Care Network

When vital readings fall outside safe clinical parameters, the Reliv ecosystem doesn't leave users stranded. It seamlessly connects them with partner healthcare providers, accredited clinics, and verified doctors for timely intervention.
