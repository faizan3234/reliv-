OUR STORY & VISION

Making preventive healthcare transparent and accessible.

Reliv was created with one simple mission: empower people to understand their essential body metrics in under three minutes, without fear, friction, or expensive clinic visits.

Why Reliv Matters

Lifestyle conditions like hypertension, cardiovascular anomalies, and metabolic imbalances often develop silently without early symptoms. Conventional diagnostics require appointment booking, travel, blood draws, and waiting days for laboratory reports.

Reliv bridges this gap by placing smart, non-invasive health ATMs in everyday environments: college campuses, workplaces, gyms, and public transit nodes. A user steps up, selects their preferred native language (English, Hindi, or Bengali), follows intuitive voice-guided steps, and receives an instant digital wellness summary on their mobile via QR code.
