OUR MISSION · DPIIT RECOGNISED HEALTHCARE INNOVATION

Making everyday health screening easier to access.

In India, over 70% of fatal health events stem from undetected lifestyle markers — hypertension, oxygen drops, and metabolic irregularities. Reliv transforms healthcare from reactive illness treatment to proactive daily wellness through autonomous 3-minute health checkup stations.

DPIIT Certified · DIPP287544 · Startup India · Ministry of Commerce & Industry
ARCHITECTURE

Six pillars. One unified preventive continuum.

From hardware screening to mobile intelligence and verified clinical follow-up — how the Reliv ecosystem connects everyday life with clinical peace of mind.

01

Smart Health Kiosk

Autonomous physical screening station measuring Blood Pressure, SpO₂, Pulse Rate, Temperature, Height, Weight, BMI & Snellen eye checkup in 3 minutes.

02

Campus & Corporate Hubs

Installed where students and employees live and work — active at premier institutions including IEM Gurukul Campus, Salt Lake Sector V, Kolkata.

03

Multilingual Guided Support

On-screen guidance and spoken prompts in English, Hindi and Bengali, where supported by the installed kiosk.

04

Instant Digital Health Card

Where supported, the kiosk can offer QR-based access to an optional digital summary through the Reliv companion web app.

05

Automated Health Kits

24/7 automated dispenser for emergency First-Aid Kits, Women's Wellness essentials, and travel hygiene kits right at the kiosk.

06

Appropriate Next Steps

Screening results provide useful context, not a diagnosis. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional for help interpreting concerning readings.

Reliv exists to make everyday health screening easier to access in places where people already study, work and gather. A check-in can encourage awareness, but it never replaces professional medical care.
