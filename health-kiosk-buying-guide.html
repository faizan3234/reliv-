How to choose a health kiosk in India.

A useful health kiosk proposal starts with your people, your venue and the checks you need. Use this guide to compare scope, total cost and ongoing support before choosing a Health ATM, workplace wellness station or fitness kiosk.

Prepared by Reliv Medicare Private Limited, the Kolkata company behind Reliv Smart Health Kiosk.

1. Define what the kiosk needs to do

Workplaces & campuses

Decide who can use the station, expected daily usage, whether assistance is needed, and how people receive their own results.

Workplace screening · Campus wellness

Gyms & fitness spaces

Separate routine measurements such as height, weight and calculated BMI from optional body-composition features. Ask which measurements your proposed configuration actually includes.

Fitness and wellness kiosks

Community venues

Plan accessibility, opening hours, a private place to view results, and who will assist visitors. Confirm venue permission before announcing public availability.

Explore Reliv stations

“Health kiosk” and “Health ATM” describe a category. Different products can have very different hardware and services. Compare the written scope rather than the label or the number of advertised parameters alone.

2. Ask for a like-for-like proposal

Questions to ask every supplier, including Reliv

MeasurementsIncluded sensors, directly measured values, calculated estimates, optional modules and the exact configuration shown in the demonstration.

3. Compare the total cost, not just the machine

Ask each supplier to quote the same configuration, venue and contract period. Keep one-time costs separate from recurring costs and per-session charges so a purchase quote can be compared with a monthly plan.

One-time costs

Machine and selected modules, delivery, installation, site preparation and initial training, where charged.

Recurring costs

Lease or subscription, connectivity, maintenance, calibration and support, where applicable to the agreement.

Usage costs

Any per-checkup or report charges, consumables and payment-related charges. Confirm who pays each cost.

Reliv pricing is available on request. A current numeric starting price has not been published. A machine purchase price, monthly deployment fee and individual checkup charge are different prices. Request the relevant Reliv quote.

4. Plan the installation and handover

  1. Share your site: city, venue type, intended users, expected usage and the space available.
  2. Confirm requirements: ask for the selected model's dimensions, power and connectivity needs, accessibility requirements and environmental conditions.
  3. Agree responsibilities: name a venue contact and confirm who handles training, routine cleaning, maintenance, payment queries and technical support.
  4. Check the handover: compare installed features with the signed proposal, try the user journey, confirm report access and record the support contact.
  5. Review actual use: track completed sessions, interruptions and user feedback. Follow the agreed privacy arrangements when reviewing usage.

Health kiosk buying questions

Can I buy or lease a Reliv health kiosk?

Reliv accepts enquiries about purchase and monthly plans for organisations. Ask for current availability, the included configuration, commercial terms and support scope before committing.

What is the starting price of a Reliv kiosk?

Reliv has not published a numeric starting price. Request a written quote for your city, configuration and purchase or monthly plan. Confirm GST, delivery, installation and recurring charges separately.

Does a larger parameter count mean a better kiosk?

A parameter count alone does not describe the hardware or service included. Ask which values are directly measured, which are calculated and which require optional modules, consumables or separate services. Match that scope to your intended use.

What should I send when requesting a quote?

Share your organisation, city, venue type, expected daily usage, number of kiosks, required features, purchase or monthly-plan preference and target installation period. Include a contact name, phone and email for the proposal.
