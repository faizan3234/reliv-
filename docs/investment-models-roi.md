## 1. The 3 Business Investment Models & Live Calculations

### 💰 Model 1: Outright Purchase (Buy — ₹1.2 Lakhs)
**The Deal:**
- One-time capital investment with complete machine ownership.
- Zero internet charges, zero hosting charges forever.
- 100% of checkup revenue goes directly into the partner’s pocket.
- Eligible for Section 32 IT Act depreciation tax benefits on medical/screening equipment.

**Live User Example Calculation:**
- Checkup fee: Set at **₹30 per user** (partner can adjust freely).
- Footfall: Conservative estimate of **20 users/day**.
- Daily Revenue: `20 users × ₹30 = ₹600 / day`.
- Monthly Profit: `₹600 × 30 days = ₹18,000 / month pure profit`.
- Annual Profit: `₹18,000 × 12 = ₹2,16,000 / year`.
- **Payback Velocity:** Full capital recovery in **~6.7 to 7 months** (`₹1,20,000 ÷ ₹18,000 = 6.67`). From Month 8 onward, 100% of collections are pure recurring profit.

---

### 🤝 Model 2: Monthly Rental (₹12,000 / month)
**The Deal:**
- Low barrier to entry with zero capital expenditure (funded out of operational expenses).
- Fixed flat rent of **₹12,000 / month**.
- Partner collects and keeps 100% of all customer checkup collections.
- Includes comprehensive hardware maintenance, regular sensor calibration, and software updates.
- 100% tax-deductible operational expense (OpEx).

**Live User Example Calculation:**
- Checkup fee: **₹30 per checkup**.
- Footfall: Moderate footfall of **30 users/day**.
- Monthly Gross Collection: `30 × ₹30 × 30 days = ₹27,000 / month`.
- Minus Kiosk Rent: `₹27,000 – ₹12,000 rent = ₹15,000 / month pure net profit`.
- Annual Profit: `₹15,000 × 12 = ₹1,80,000 / year net profit`.
- **Breakeven Threshold:** `₹12,000 ÷ ₹30 = 400 checkups / month = 13.3 checkups/day`.  
  *(At just 14 checkups a day, rent is completely covered; every checkup after that is pure profit).*

---

### 🏢 Model 3: Space & Profit Sharing (Zero-CapEx Progressive Plan)
**The Deal:**
- **₹0 upfront hardware cost. ₹0 monthly rental fee. Zero financial risk.**
- Partner only provides a **4×4 sq. ft. space** and a standard 5A electricity plug.
- Reliv handles shipping, installation, uptime, consumables, and maintenance.
- Revenue earned from kiosk checkups is split progressively over time:

| Timeline Phase | Revenue Split (Reliv / Partner) | Purpose | Monthly Partner Income (at 40 users/day @ ₹30) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First 1.5 Years** | 85% Reliv / 15% Partner *(or 60/40 starter)* | Covers initial hardware manufacturing & deployment CapEx | **₹5,400 to ₹14,400 / month** |
| **1.5 to 5 Years** | 60% Reliv / 40% Partner | Standard mature operational revenue sharing | **₹14,400 / month** |
| **After 5 Years** | 40% Reliv / 60% Partner | **Partner earns the 60% lion's share** once amortized! | **₹21,600 / month** |

**Live User Example Calculation (at 60/40 & 40/60 Phases):**
- At 40 users/day @ ₹30 = ₹1,200/day = **₹36,000/month gross**.
- **Years 1.5–5 (40% Share):** Partner earns **₹14,400/month** passively with zero capital invested (`0.40 × ₹36,000`).
- **After Year 5 (60% Share):** Partner earns **₹21,600/month** on the exact same volume (`0.60 × ₹36,000`).

## 2. Plain English Layman Explanation: The "Bank ATM" Analogy

### Why Model 3 Has ZERO Depreciation and Pure Profit for the Host:
Think of a bank installing an ATM machine in the corner of a hospital or college building:
1. **Did the college buy the ATM?** No. The bank bought it.
2. **If the keypad breaks or receipt paper jams, does the college pay?** No. The bank sends a technician to fix it.
3. **Does the college worry about the machine losing value over time (depreciation)?** No. It is not on your balance sheet; you don't own it, so depreciation is ₹0 to you.
4. **What does the college do?** They provide 4×4 sq. ft. of space and a wall socket. Every single month, a passive commission check arrives in their bank account.

**Summary:**
- **Cost to Host:** ₹0
- **Depreciation to Host:** ₹0 (Reliv absorbs 100% of wear-and-tear)
- **Maintenance to Host:** ₹0
- **Host Return:** 100% Pure Cash. If footfall dips, you never owe a single rupee.

- Truly self-service touchscreen navigation.
- **Multilingual Voice AI Guidance:** The kiosk can listen, understand, and speak fluently in **English, Hindi, and Bengali**.
- Completely self-guided — no receptionist, nurse, or technician required on-site.
- **Monthly Staffing Overhead: ₹0.**

- When idle between checkups, the high-definition display functions as an eye-level digital billboard.
- Partners can showcase their own internal campus/company notices or sell ad inventory to nearby local businesses (cafes, gyms, pharmacies, coaching centers).
- **Secondary Revenue: +₹5,000 to ₹15,000/month** in pure ad margin.

### 💊 3. Automated Medicine & Kit Dispensing Margin
- Integrated automated dispensing for emergency first-aid kits, women's wellness essentials, and preventive test kits on-demand.
- Partners purchase kits at wholesale rates from Reliv and pocket **30% to 40% retail profit margins** on every automated sale without cashier overhead.
