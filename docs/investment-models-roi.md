## 1. The 3 Business Investment Models & Live Calculations

### 💰 Model 1: Outright Purchase (Buy — ₹95,000 Only)
**The Deal:**
- **One-time capital investment of just ₹95,000** with complete machine ownership.
- **Important Clarification to Eliminate Buyer Confusion:** Many first-time buyers mistakenly think the machine costs ₹2 Lakhs or has ongoing annual costs. **The machine is ₹95,000 one-time only!** The **₹2,16,000/year is YOUR pure recurring profit**, NOT the machine price.
- Zero internet charges, zero hosting charges forever.
- 100% of checkup revenue goes directly into the partner’s pocket.
- Eligible for Section 32 IT Act depreciation tax benefits on medical/screening equipment.

**Live User Example Calculation:**
- Machine Price: **₹95,000 (one-time CapEx)**.
- Checkup fee: Set at **₹30 per user** (partner can adjust freely).
- Footfall: Conservative estimate of **20 users/day**.
- Daily Revenue: `20 users × ₹30 = ₹600 / day`.
- Monthly Profit: `₹600 × 30 days = ₹18,000 / month pure profit`.
- Annual Profit (To Partner): `₹18,000 × 12 = ₹2,16,000 / year pure net profit`.
- **Payback Velocity:** Full capital recovery in **~5.3 months** (`₹95,000 ÷ ₹18,000 = 5.27 months`). From Month 6 onward, 100% of collections are pure profit for life with zero recurring device fees!

### 🤝 Model 2: Monthly Rental (₹12,000 / month)
**The Deal:**
- Low barrier to entry with zero capital expenditure (funded out of operational expenses).
- Fixed flat rent of **₹12,000 / month**.
- Partner collects and keeps 100% of all customer checkup collections.
- Includes comprehensive hardware maintenance, regular sensor calibration, and software updates.
- 100% tax-deductible operational expense (OpEx).

**Live User Example Calculation:**
- Checkup fee: **₹30 per checkup**.
- Footfall: Moderate footfall of **30 users/day**.
- Monthly Gross Collection: `30 × ₹30 × 30 days = ₹27,000 / month`.
- Minus Kiosk Rent: `₹27,000 – ₹12,000 rent = ₹15,000 / month pure net profit`.
- Annual Profit: `₹15,000 × 12 = ₹1,80,000 / year net profit`.
- **Breakeven Threshold:** `₹12,000 ÷ ₹30 = 400 checkups / month = 13.3 checkups/day`.  
  *(At just 14 checkups a day, rent is completely covered; every checkup after that is pure profit).*

## 2. Plain English Layman Explanation: The "Bank ATM" Analogy

### Why Model 3 Has ZERO Depreciation and Pure Profit for the Host:
Think of a bank installing an ATM machine in the corner of a hospital or college building:
1. **Did the college buy the ATM?** No. The bank bought it.
2. **If the keypad breaks or receipt paper jams, does the college pay?** No. The bank sends a technician to fix it.
3. **Does the college worry about the machine losing value over time (depreciation)?** No. It is not on your balance sheet; you don't own it, so depreciation is ₹0 to you.
4. **What does the college do?** They provide 4×4 sq. ft. of space and a wall socket. Every single month, a passive commission check arrives in their bank account.

**Summary:**
- **Cost to Host:** ₹0
- **Depreciation to Host:** ₹0 (Reliv absorbs 100% of wear-and-tear)
- **Maintenance to Host:** ₹0
- **Host Return:** 100% Pure Cash. If footfall dips, you never owe a single rupee.
