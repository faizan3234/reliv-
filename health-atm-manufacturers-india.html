The Evolution of Health ATMs in India

As India expands its digital health infrastructure under the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM), Health ATMs (smart diagnostic kiosks) have emerged as critical point-of-care tools. Deployed across corporate tech parks, university campuses, primary health centers (PHCs), railway stations, and retail pharmacies, these kiosks provide fast, walk-in vital screening without requiring hospital OPD queues.

Key Evaluation Criteria for Kiosk Buyers

1. Operational Independence

Does the machine require a full-time nurse or technician, or is it 100% self-directed with natural Voice AI?

2. Capital Expenditure (CapEx)

Are machines priced at ₹4L–₹8L+, or can you acquire hardware at disruptive rates like Reliv’s ₹95,000 CapEx?

3. Sensor Accuracy & Speed

Are vital measurements (BP, pulse, SpO₂, temperature, BMI) captured within 3 minutes with clear visual guidance?

4. Footprint & Installation

Does the unit require plumbing and heavy alterations, or a compact 4×4 sq. ft. plug-and-play installation?
