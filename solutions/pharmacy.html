How a Reliv Health ATM Can Support Your Pharmacy

  • Offer Convenient Vital Checks: Customers can access blood pressure, pulse, SpO2, and temperature checks. Validate local demand before choosing a commercial plan.
  • Support Informed Follow-up: Screening results are not a diagnosis or prescription. Plan a referral pathway to a qualified clinician; do not use a kiosk reading alone to recommend or change medication.
  • Guided Self-Service: Touchscreens and voice guidance in English, Hindi, and Bengali support users. Plan for cleaning, assistance, accessibility, maintenance and escalation at your venue.
  • Ultra-Compact 4×4 Sq. Ft. Footprint: Designed to fit cleanly in a retail pharmacy corner or near the prescription waiting area with standard 220V plug-and-play setup.
  • Illustrative Revenue: At ₹30 per checkup, 30 paid checks/day and 30 operating days, gross collections would be ₹27,000/month. After ₹12,000 rent, ₹15,000 remains before other costs and taxes. This is an example, not a forecast or net profit.
