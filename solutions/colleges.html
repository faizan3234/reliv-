CAMPUS HEALTH & WELLNESS SOLUTIONS

Empowering students and faculty with preventive healthcare.

University life brings intense academic pressure, erratic sleep, and changing diets. Reliv helps universities cultivate a culture of proactive health awareness through instant 3-minute self-service screenings.

Why Educational Institutions Choose Reliv

  • Proven Live Campus Deployment: Installed and actively used at IEM Kolkata (Gurukul Campus), serving thousands of engineering students and faculty.
  • Nationally Recognized MedTech: Winner of Medverse 2026 Excellence in MedTech Device Startup and officially recognized by the Government of India (DPIIT · DIPP287544 under Startup India).
  • Zero Staffing Burden: The kiosk is fully autonomous with intuitive voice-assisted navigation in English, Hindi, and Bengali. No clinical operator required on-site.
  • Turnkey Institutional Maintenance: Reliv handles hardware delivery, calibration, sensor consumables, and ongoing software updates.
  • Campus Accreditation & CSR: Contributes directly to institutional student welfare and wellness compliance metrics.
