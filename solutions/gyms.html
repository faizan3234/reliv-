FITNESS & ATHLETIC WELLNESS

Reliv fitness and wellness kiosks for gyms.

Bring guided health screening into your gym with Reliv Kiosk. Available checks include blood pressure, SpO₂, pulse, temperature, height, weight and calculated BMI. Body-composition estimates are optional and depend on the installed configuration; confirm the available metrics before choosing your kiosk.

Plan a useful member check-in

Choose a suitable space for guided measurements and agree on cleaning, maintenance and support responsibilities. Screening readings support awareness; they are not exercise clearance or a diagnosis.

Understand the measurements

Height and weight are measured; BMI is calculated. If body-composition outputs are included in your configuration, treat them as estimates and ask which method produces each value. See the Reliv measurement guide.

Pricing and setup

Request current fitness kiosk pricing with your city, expected daily use and preferred purchase or monthly plan. Screening fees for individual gym members are separate from the cost of the machine.
