Medical & Diagnostic Disclaimer

Please read this notice carefully before using Reliv health screening stations.

Automated Wellness Summary — Not a Clinical Diagnosis

Reliv provides automated preventive screening indicators. The digital summary, readings, and plain-language interpretations generated by the device are for educational and preventive wellness tracking only. They do not constitute a medical diagnosis, clinical prescription, or treatment advice.

Emergency Care

If you are experiencing acute chest pain, shortness of breath, severe dizziness, numbness, or any acute medical emergency, do not wait for a kiosk checkup. Contact emergency medical services or proceed immediately to the nearest hospital emergency room.

Consulting Healthcare Providers

A single reading does not tell a complete medical story. Always consult a licensed physician, cardiologist, or qualified healthcare specialist for the interpretation of your vital signs and before starting or changing any prescription medications, fitness regimes, or medical treatments.
