Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Principles of Health Data Privacy

At Reliv, we believe personal health data belongs exclusively to the individual. Health metrics captured during a kiosk screening session (such as blood pressure, pulse, oxygen saturation, and body composition) are used solely to generate the participant's immediate digital summary.

2. Website Demo and Live Product Data

The public website demonstration uses illustrative readings; it is not a live health record. Physical kiosk screenings, QR-based report access and the companion app are separate services and may handle session information to provide the requested features. Please contact Reliv for details of any collection, sharing, retention and deletion practices that apply to the actual kiosk and app. Do not infer live-system data handling from the website demo.

3. No Sale of Health Data

Reliv does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade identifiable individual health readings with third-party advertisers, insurance providers, or brokers.

4. Contacting Our Data Privacy Officer

For questions regarding data practices, write to relivcustomercare.in@gmail.com.
