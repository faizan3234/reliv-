How a Health ATM Works in 3 Simple Steps

1. Multilingual Authentication: The user touches the screen, picks their native language (English, Hindi, or Bengali), and registers basic demographics (age and biological sex to calibrate standard health reference intervals).

2. Guided Sensor Readings: Natural voice instructions guide the user step-by-step: using the blood pressure cuff, placing a finger on the pulse oximeter sensor, and following the height and weight measurement instructions for the installed configuration.

3. Instant Paperless Digital Summary: A typical Reliv screening takes about three minutes, depending on the checks selected. Where digital reports are available, follow the kiosk’s instructions to access your summary. Review any payment steps before proceeding.

Frequently Asked Questions About Health ATMs

What is a Health ATM?

A Health ATM is an automated, self-service medical diagnostic kiosk equipped with clinical-grade sensors. Similar to how a bank ATM dispenses cash, a Health ATM allows users to independently check and receive their vital health parameters—such as blood pressure, pulse, SpO₂, temperature, and BMI—within 3 minutes.

What tests can a Health ATM perform?

Core parameters measured at a standard smart Health ATM include Blood Pressure (systolic/diastolic), Oxygen Saturation (SpO₂), Pulse Rate, Body Temperature, Height, Weight, and Body Mass Index (BMI). Some configurations also feature body composition analysis and digital report generation.

How long does a Health ATM screening take?

A complete screening session typically takes around 3 minutes. The user is guided step-by-step through interactive touchscreens and Voice AI guidance in their local language (English, Hindi, or Bengali).

Where can Health ATMs be deployed?

Health ATMs can be installed in college and university campuses, corporate offices, gyms and fitness centers, hospital OPD waiting areas, retail pharmacies, shopping malls, and public transit hubs.
