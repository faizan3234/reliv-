What is a Health ATM?

Just as a banking ATM makes cash deposits and withdrawals accessible anytime without waiting in branch queues, a Health ATM makes essential health vital checks self-service and instant.

🏛️ DPIIT Recognized & Certified (DIPP287544) 🏆 Medverse 2026 Winner (Excellence in MedTech Device) 📜 Startup India Certified

How a Health ATM Works in 3 Simple Steps

1. Multilingual Authentication: The user touches the screen, picks their native language (English, Hindi, or Bengali), and registers basic demographics (age and biological sex to calibrate standard health reference intervals).

2. Guided Sensor Readings: Natural voice instructions guide the user step-by-step: inserting an arm into the automated blood pressure cuff, placing a finger on the pulse oximeter sensor, and standing barefoot on the bio-impedance footpads.

3. Instant Paperless Digital Summary: The Health ATM processes all signals in under 3 minutes and outputs an encrypted QR code on the display. Scanning it opens the user's personal wellness summary on their smartphone.
