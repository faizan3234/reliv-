PASSIVE REVENUE MULTIPLIERS

Zero Effort, Maximum Profit: Three Built-In Perks

Your earnings are not limited to checkup fees alone. Reliv kiosks are engineered with secondary passive revenue streams and total operational independence.

100% Autonomous (Zero Staff Cost)

Truly self-service touchscreen navigation powered by multilingual Voice AI guidance in English, Hindi, and Bengali.

  • Listens, understands and speaks fluently
  • No receptionist, nurse or technician required
  • Zero monthly payroll overhead for venue

Digital Screen Ad Monetization

When idle between checkups, the high-definition display functions as an eye-level digital billboard in your high-footfall space.

  • Showcase your own institution promotions
  • Sell ad slots to local cafes, gyms, pharmacies
  • Earn +₹5,000 to ₹10,000/mo extra ad revenue

Automated Medicine & Kit Margins

Integrated automated dispensing for emergency first-aid, women's wellness essentials, and preventive test kits on-demand.

  • Buy kits at wholesale prices from Reliv
  • Pocket 30% to 40% retail margin automatically
  • Zero cashier or inventory staff needed
