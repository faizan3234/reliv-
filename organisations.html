THE FINANCIAL BLUEPRINT

Why each model works financially

Healthcare screening stations enjoy strong unit economics because vitals checks are high-frequency, non-invasive, and cost almost nothing in consumables. Here is the mathematical reality behind each approach.

Model 01 · CapEx

Outright Purchase Maths

Best for institutions with available capital looking to maximize long-term balance sheet returns.

Daily Volume (Moderate)60 checks / day
Ticket Price₹70 / check
Monthly Gross Revenue₹1,26,000 / mo
Electricity & Upkeep (~50W)-₹3,000 / mo
Net Monthly Profit₹1,23,000 / mo
  • Full capital recovery in ~3–5 months: At 60 checks/day, the entire kiosk cost is repaid in one semester.
  • 3-Year Projected Return: Generates ~₹40.7 Lakhs in net profit against a one-time ~₹3.5L investment (>1,100% ROI).
  • Tax Depreciation: Accelerates tax savings via Sec 32 medical/screening equipment depreciation.
