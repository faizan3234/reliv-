Frequently Asked Questions — Health ATM & Kiosk Pricing in India

How much does a Health ATM machine cost in India?

In India, Health ATM machine prices typically range from ₹2,50,000 to ₹8,00,000+. However, Reliv offers an outright purchase option at ₹95,000 only (one-time CapEx with zero recurring hosting or internet charges), as well as a ₹12,000/month flat rental model, and a zero-Capex Space & Profit Sharing model for high-footfall spaces.

How much profit can a Health ATM generate per month?

A Health ATM generates robust profit across three streams: (1) Vital checkups: at a conservative ₹30 checkup fee with 20 users/day, an outright owner earns ₹18,000/month pure net profit (₹2,16,000/year, recovering total device cost in ~5.3 months); (2) Digital screen advertising monetization; (3) Automated wellness kit and OTC medicine dispensing margins.

Can I get a Health ATM installed in my space with zero upfront investment?

Yes. Reliv offers a Space & Profit Sharing model exclusively for high-footfall public venues, transit hubs, hospitals, and college campuses. The partner provides a 4×4 sq. ft. footprint and electricity with ₹0 hardware cost, earning progressive monthly profit share (starting at 80-20 Reliv/Partner, shifting every 2.5 years up to 50-50).

Does a smart health kiosk require a full-time operator or nurse?

No. Reliv smart health kiosks are 100% autonomous and self-service. Built-in interactive touchscreens and Voice AI guidance fluently assist users in English, Hindi, and Bengali, eliminating operator salary overhead.

What is the starting price of a Reliv health kiosk?

Reliv provides current pricing on request for the selected kiosk configuration and installation location. Machine purchase is ₹95,000 one-time, monthly rental is ₹12,000/month, and Space Sharing is ₹0 upfront. Ask the team for a written quote that confirms taxes, installation and recurring charges.

Is the kiosk purchase price the same as a checkup price?

No. A purchase quote covers an organisation’s kiosk deployment. A checkup or digital-report charge applies to an individual session at a station.

Can I lease a Reliv kiosk?

Contact Reliv about a monthly plan and confirm availability, lease duration, maintenance and support terms for your venue. Flat rent is ₹12,000/month with zero capital expenditure.

Are health checkups free?

Charges depend on the station and service selected. Review the displayed screening, report and activation charges before payment.
