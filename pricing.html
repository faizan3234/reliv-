Pricing questions

What is the starting price of a Reliv health kiosk?

Reliv provides current pricing on request for the selected kiosk configuration and installation location. Ask the team for a written quote that confirms taxes, installation and recurring charges.

Is the kiosk purchase price the same as a checkup price?

No. A purchase quote covers an organisation’s kiosk deployment. A checkup or digital-report charge applies to an individual session at a station.

Can I lease a Reliv kiosk?

Contact Reliv about a monthly plan and confirm availability, lease duration, maintenance and support terms for your venue.

Are health checkups free?

Charges depend on the station and service selected. Review the displayed screening, report and activation charges before payment.
