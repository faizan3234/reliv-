Reliv Kiosk: smart health screening in India.

Reliv Smart Health Kiosk, also called the Reliv Health ATM, is a self-service screening station from Reliv Medicare Private Limited in Kolkata, India. It brings guided health checks to campuses, workplaces, gyms and community spaces.

Check blood pressure, oxygen saturation (SpO₂), pulse, temperature, height and weight, with calculated BMI. A typical session takes about three minutes, depending on the selected checks and setup. Guidance is available in English, Hindi and Bengali.

What does the Reliv kiosk check?

Measurements, calculations and optional features have different meanings. Confirm the exact configuration before booking a deployment or travelling to a station.

CheckHow to understand it
Blood pressureA cuff measures systolic and diastolic blood pressure. Follow the station’s positioning and rest instructions.
SpO₂ and pulseA pulse oximeter provides oxygen saturation and pulse readings.
TemperatureA contactless infrared sensor provides a temperature reading.
Height and weightThese measurements are the inputs for calculating body mass index.
BMIA calculated indicator based on height and weight; it is not a separate sensor test.
Optional featuresEyesight screening, body-composition estimates and digital-report access depend on the installed configuration. Ask for the included feature list.

Reliv provides screening information for awareness. It does not provide a medical diagnosis or replace professional medical advice. A parameter count should not be interpreted as an equivalent number of independent medical tests.

Reliv kiosk questions

What is Reliv Kiosk?

Reliv Kiosk is a self-service health screening kiosk, also called a Health ATM, from Reliv Medicare Private Limited in Kolkata, India. It supports everyday health, fitness and wellness awareness.

What does Reliv measure?

Core checks include blood pressure, SpO₂, pulse, temperature, height and weight, with calculated BMI. Optional eyesight screening, body-composition estimates and report access depend on the installed configuration.

What is the starting price of a Reliv kiosk?

Contact Reliv for current purchase or monthly plan pricing for your configuration and installation location. Checkup charges are separate and are displayed at the station before payment.

Is a Health ATM the same as a Reliv kiosk?

Health ATM is a general term for self-service health screening stations. Reliv Smart Health Kiosk is one product in this category; capabilities vary between products and configurations.

Does Reliv provide a medical diagnosis?

No. Reliv provides screening information for awareness and does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
