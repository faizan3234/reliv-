SCREENING FEATURES

What does the Reliv Health Kiosk measure?

Guided, non-invasive checks for everyday screening. Available sensors depend on the kiosk configuration.

01

Vital Signs Monitoring

Oscillometric blood pressure (systolic & diastolic), optical pulse oximetry (SpO₂), pulse rate, and body surface temperature.

02

Body Composition Metrics

Body-composition estimates such as body fat, muscle mass and body water, when supported by the installed configuration. These are not diagnostic tests.

03

Instant Digital Report

Where a digital report is offered, follow the kiosk instructions and any displayed access or pricing steps to view the summary on your phone.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Smart Health Kiosk?

A smart health kiosk is a self-service medical screening station that combines measurement sensors, an interactive touchscreen, and guided software to measure vital signs and body metrics without needing a nurse or technician present.

How long does a full checkup take?

A standard checkup takes approximately 3 minutes from language selection to receiving your digital QR summary.

Does it provide clinical medical diagnosis?

No. The Reliv Health Kiosk provides an automated preventive wellness summary and health risk indicators. It is not a clinical medical diagnosis or a replacement for consultation with a licensed physician.
