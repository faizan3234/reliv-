What is Reliv?

Everyday health checks shouldn't require long clinic queues or expensive lab appointments. Reliv brings self-service healthcare right where you live, study, and work.

  • Instant vital screening — Check your blood pressure, pulse rate, oxygen saturation (SpO₂), body temperature, and body composition in about 3 minutes.
  • Essential health kits — Automated dispensing of First-Aid Kits, Women's Wellness Kits, and Travel Kits for daily campus and workplace needs.
  • DPIIT Recognized & Award-Winning — Certified under Startup India (DIPP287544) and winner of The Oscars of Healthcare (Medverse Salem) for Excellence in MedTech.
  • Built for everyday venues — Reliv kiosks are designed for campuses, workplaces and other eligible partner locations; check the locations page for listed installations.

YOUR PAYMENT COMPANION

How to install & use the Reliv app

The Reliv companion is a Progressive Web App (PWA) you can open in your browser or add to your home screen. Installation steps depend on your device and browser.

iPhone Safari Setup Walkthrough Watch how to add the PWA to your home screen
  1. Step 1 — Open in Safari Open Safari on your iPhone and visit reliv7.vercel.app, or scan the QR code below.
  2. Step 2 — Tap the Browser Menu Tap the three-dots or the Safari Share icon at the bottom of your screen.
  3. Step 3 — Choose 'Add to Home Screen' Scroll down the share sheet and tap + Add to Home Screen.
  4. Step 4 — Tap 'Add' to Finish Confirm the app title and tap Add in the top-right corner. Reliv is now on your home screen!
  5. Step 5 — Scan Kiosk Payment QR If your kiosk displays a payment QR, open the app, tap Scan kiosk payment QR and follow the checkout and activation prompts.
Android Chrome Setup & Scan Walkthrough Watch how to install and scan kiosk QR codes
  1. Step 1 — Open in Chrome Open Google Chrome on your phone and go to reliv7.vercel.app.
  2. Step 2 — Tap 'Install' Chrome will show an Install Reliv banner at the top. Tap it, or tap the three dots and select Install app.
  3. Step 3 — App Added to Home Screen The standalone Reliv icon will appear directly on your home screen and in your apps list.
  4. Step 4 — Scan Kiosk Payment QR If a payment QR is shown on your kiosk, open the app, choose Scan kiosk payment QR, and follow the on-screen checkout instructions.

AUTONOMOUS CHECKUP

How the 3-minute checkup works at the kiosk

Follow the on-screen prompts at your kiosk. Checks, availability, time and any payment steps can differ by location and configuration.

1

Choose your language

Touch the screen to select English, हिंदी (Hindi), or বাংলা (Bengali). Gentle voice prompts and animated visual guides walk you through every step.

2

Rest arm & take readings

Follow the cuff and finger-sensor instructions shown on the kiosk. Available measurements and screening time depend on the installed configuration.

3

View readings and report options

Review the readings presented on screen. Where a digital report or Health QR is offered, follow the kiosk instructions; any relevant price is shown before payment.
